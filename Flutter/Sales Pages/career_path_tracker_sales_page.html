🚀 The Problem we Solve

Job hunting is chaotic. Between dozens of applications, technical interview rounds, and the constant need to upskill, most candidates lose track of their progress. Spreadsheets are clunky, and generic notes apps lack the structure you need to succeed. CareerPath was built to eliminate this chaos. It's designed to give you a clinical, data-driven view of your recruitment journey.

💎 The Glassmorphic Advantage

We believe that an app you use every day should be beautiful. Our state-of-the-art Glassmorphism UI isn't just for show—it reduces cognitive load and makes navigation intuitive. By visualising your "Today's Focus" alongside your "Interview Timeline", we help you stay in the 'Flow State' required for elite performance.

🏗️ Built for Scalability

Under the hood, CareerPath uses a production-grade Local SQLite Database. This means your data is yours—completely private, lightning-fast, and available offline. Whether you're on a plane or in a basement office, your preparation never stops.

🎯 Targeted Success

Our unique Company-Type DNA feature allows you to segment your preparation. Product companies test differently than Service giants. CareerPath helps you recognize these patterns, so you can adjust your interview strategy and technical focus based on the specific DNA of your target employer.
