Powerful MLM Features

Everything you need to run a successful MLM investment business with automated processes and real-time tracking

🌳

Binary Tree Genealogy

Complete binary tree implementation with left/right placement, visual tree representation, level tracking, and sponsor-downline relationships. Auto-balancing and spillover support.

💵

Multi-Wallet System

Three separate wallets: E-Wallet for deposits/withdrawals, Investment Wallet for active investments, and Earnings Wallet for commissions and profits. Full transaction history for each.

📊

Investment Management

Multiple investment plans with configurable returns, durations, and minimum amounts. Track active, matured, and cancelled investments with detailed P&L statements.

🎁

Commission System

Automated commission calculation with multiple types: direct referral, level commissions, binary matching, and performance bonuses. Real-time commission tracking and payouts.

📱

OTP Authentication

Secure phone-based OTP authentication with support for Twilio, MSG91, and 2Factor. Fallback to password login and session management with JWT tokens.

🔐

KYC & Bank Details

Complete KYC document upload and verification system. Bank account management for withdrawals with admin approval workflow and document storage.

📈

Daily Profit Calculation

Automated daily profit calculation based on investment plans. Cron job support for scheduled processing, profit distribution, and investment maturity handling.

💳

Deposit & Withdrawal

User-friendly deposit and withdrawal system with admin approval workflow. Transaction tracking, status updates, and automatic wallet balance updates.

👥

Team & Referral System

View your complete team hierarchy, track referrals, monitor team performance, and see earnings from your network. Unique referral codes for each user.

🎫

Support Ticket System

Built-in support ticket system for user queries and issues. Category-based tickets, priority levels, admin responses, and status tracking.

⚙️

Admin Panel

Comprehensive admin panel (Mobile & Web) for managing users, investments, approvals, commissions, and system settings. Real-time analytics and reporting.

📊

Dashboard & Analytics

Beautiful dashboard with real-time statistics, investment overview, earnings summary, team performance, and graphical representations of your MLM business.
